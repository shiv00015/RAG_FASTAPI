Once upon a time, a lion was sleeping in the forest when a small mouse accidentally ran over his nose. The lion woke up, caught the mouse, and was about to kill it. 
The mouse begged the lion to let him go, promising to be grateful one day. The lion found it funny that a tiny mouse could ever help him, so he let it go. 
A few days later, the lion was walking in the forest when he got caught in a hunter's net. 
He roared loudly, but he couldn't get free. Suddenly, he heard the little mouse. The mouse and his friends came and started to chew through the ropes of the net. 
Soon, the lion was free. He thanked the mouse and they became friends.
